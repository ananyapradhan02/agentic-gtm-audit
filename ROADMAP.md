# roadmap

1. team mode: paste several teammates' share links and see where answers disagree per question. disagreement on icp or pricing is the finding.
2. benchmark: compare your area scores with an illustrative, clearly labelled dataset of agentic-ai vendors (built from public pricing and homepage pages, each with a source and date).
3. teardown mode: paste a homepage's copy and get a scripted classification (leads with technology, promise, job done or job plus proof), with the rule that fired shown.
4. show the scoring rules: a short "how this is scored" section under the scorecard with the thresholds and the move rule, so a team that argues with a stamp can see why it fired.
5. a title block on the printed report (team name typed on the page, kept only in the url), so a stack of printouts from one offsite can be told apart.
